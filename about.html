About Invelsoft

Invelsoft LLP is a Microsoft partner. We sell genuine Microsoft 365, Office and Windows licenses and make sure they're installed, activated and working for you.

Who we are

Invelsoft is a technology company and Microsoft partner serving customers in the United States. We help people and businesses choose the right Microsoft license, buy or renew it, and get it set up properly — with real people available to help at any time.

What we offer

  • Microsoft 365 Personal, Family and Business subscriptions
  • Office Home 2024 and other one-time Office licenses
  • Windows 11 Home and Pro licenses
  • Renewals of existing Microsoft 365 subscriptions
  • Free installation, activation and Outlook setup with every license
  • Ongoing 24/7 support from our US-based team

How we work

  • You tell us what you need through our form, chat or phone.
  • A specialist recommends the right license and confirms the price before you buy. You are never under any obligation to purchase.
  • After purchase, we install and activate your software. We only connect to your computer remotely with your explicit permission, and you can end the session at any time.
