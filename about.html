About Invelsoft

Invelsoft LLP helps people and businesses buy, set up and troubleshoot Microsoft software, from Office 365 and Windows to Outlook, Excel and Xbox.

Who we are

Invelsoft is a technology services company. We resell Microsoft software licenses and subscriptions and provide independent technical support for the products we work with. Our goal is simple: get your software working the way it should, and explain things clearly along the way.

What we help with

  • Buying or renewing Microsoft software licenses and subscriptions
  • Installing and setting up Office 365, Windows 10 and Windows 11
  • Activation and product key problems
  • Outlook email setup, sync and data file errors
  • Excel files that won't open, crash or behave unexpectedly
  • Xbox console setup, account sign-in and connection issues

How we work

  • You choose your product, describe the issue and tell us when to call.
  • A support specialist calls you back and talks through the problem.
  • If a paid service or product is needed, we explain the cost before any work begins. You are never under any obligation to buy.
  • We only connect to your computer remotely with your explicit permission, and you can end the session at any time.
